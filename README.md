# 🛒 Blinkit Grocery Sales Analysis

An end-to-end **Business Intelligence** project that analyzes Blinkit's grocery sales data using **Python**, **PostgreSQL**, and **Power BI**. The project follows a complete analytics workflow—from data preprocessing and exploratory data analysis to SQL-based business insights and interactive dashboard development.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

Blinkit aims to better understand its grocery sales performance, outlet efficiency, and product demand across different locations. The objective is to identify sales trends, evaluate outlet performance, analyze customer preferences, and generate actionable insights that support inventory planning and business growth.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Evaluate outlet performance by type, size, and location.
- Measure customer satisfaction using ratings.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Generate business recommendations based on data.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

- Medium-sized outlets generated the highest sales.
- Tier 3 outlets contributed significantly to total revenue.
- Supermarket Type1 achieved the highest sales.
- Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were among the best-selling categories.
- Customer ratings remained consistently high.
- Both Low Fat and Regular products showed strong demand.

---

# 📌 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-demand product categories.
- Expand operations in high-performing outlet locations.
- Optimize inventory allocation based on outlet size.
- Improve the performance of lower-performing outlets.
- Use dashboard insights for continuous business monitoring.
